How a website programme runs

Same discipline as our Zoho work: discover, design, agree, then build.

1. Brief

Audience, pages, proof, and the action you want visitors to take.

2. Mockups

Two or three clickable directions. You pick one. We do not mix all three.

3. Build

Production site: real content, forms, SEO, and mobile — on the signed-off design.

4. Handover

Hosting, analytics, and a walkthrough so your team can update what they need.
