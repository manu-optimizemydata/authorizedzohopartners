The Problem

Why Manufacturing Operations Break Down at Scale

Most factories outgrow patchwork systems long before they outgrow demand. These are the gaps Zoho ERP is designed to close.

01 Workforce

Deskless teams, high compliance load

Shop-floor staff, contract workers, and shift teams often lack email or desktop access - yet attendance, safety records, and payroll accuracy still depend on manual tracking.

02 Operations

Shift complexity and planning friction

Rotating shifts, overtime rules, production schedules, and multi-location coordination are hard to manage in spreadsheets without delays and errors.

03 Compliance

Statutory and audit pressure

PF, ESI, professional tax, labour registers, and location-specific rules create filing risk when payroll and HR data live in separate files.

04 Integration

Siloed data across departments

When gates, stores, production, and finance do not share one record, costing drifts, stock decisions lag, and leadership lacks a reliable daily picture.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Zoho ERP for manufacturing?

Zoho ERP brings production planning, inventory, procurement, HR, payroll, expenses, and accounting into one connected cloud platform - so manufacturing teams work from shared data instead of separate files.

Can Zoho ERP support multiple plants?

Yes. You can configure parent-child org structures with plant-level shifts, attendance rules, and reporting while leadership keeps a consolidated view.

Does it work with biometric attendance?

Zoho People supports integrations with common biometric devices so gate data can feed attendance and payroll with less manual entry.

How long does implementation take?

Most manufacturing rollouts span 8 to 16 weeks depending on plant count, module scope, and data migration complexity. We use phased delivery to reduce disruption.

Can shop-floor workers use the system without full licenses?

Yes. Employee records can be maintained for workers who do not need full application access, with mobile self-service for payslips, leave, and help requests where appropriate.
