Travel & Tourism Case Study · Luxury Hospitality

How Zoho One became the operating system for a global boutique travel brand

A premium online travel business connects guests to hotels, resorts, and private villas across more than 100 countries. After outgrowing a patchwork of free tools, the team standardised sales, marketing, and support on Zoho.

The business

Founded by two entrepreneurs with deep roots in tourism, the company began as a remote-first venture built around curated luxury stays. Early inspiration came from spotting a gap in high-quality booking platforms while attending a business retreat overseas.

The website launched in late 2013. While revenue grew gradually, the founders valued location independence - working across regions in Asia and beyond without tying operations to a single office. When global travel paused in 2020, the team consolidated operations, streamlined staffing, and refocused on a simpler, more automated booking model with fewer moving parts.

The challenge

Like many growing travel startups, the business had to stretch limited budgets while still looking professional to high-end guests.

  • Early operations funded personally, pushing the team toward free software and heavy automation
  • Dozens of separate apps linked through middleware, plus email marketing and spreadsheet tracking
  • Poor fit between off-the-shelf CRM terminology and how the travel team actually worked
  • Internal Word documents needed to explain how each app's labels mapped to real workflows
  • A mid-tier CRM migration failed after outgrowing a previous marketing platform's limits
  • Need for one platform that could scale with the business instead of swapping tools every few years

What Zoho delivered

Zoho CRM became the centre of the stack, with the rest of Zoho One wrapped around bookings, service, and internal collaboration.

Zoho CRM & website integration

A tailored content-managed site feeds listings through an API. Every enquiry and completed booking lands in CRM with full contact and sales context.

  • Custom contact types instead of a traditional lead model
  • Blueprint-driven follow-up after purchase
  • Colour-coded monthly targets for bookings and departures
  • Destination coverage goals tracked in dashboards

SalesIQ, Desk & Campaigns

Guest questions are handled through live chat and structured tickets, while marketing pages and email journeys stay tied to CRM records.

  • Chatbot paths when the team is offline
  • Help centre linked to each guest profile
  • Email campaigns with open and click visibility per contact

Mail, Forms & payments

Transactional messages and gift-voucher purchases run through Zoho mail and form tools with payment integration.

  • Booking and password emails from transactional mail
  • Unified team signatures and inbox controls
  • Voucher sales creating Desk tickets and CRM records

Cliq, Meeting, Projects & Sheet

Day-to-day teamwork, board reviews, recurring ops tasks, and planning spreadsheets moved into Zoho's collaboration suite.

  • Informal chat and quick video on Cliq
  • Formal meetings on Zoho Meeting
  • Website issue backlog tracked in Projects
  • Sheets replacing separate spreadsheet subscriptions

Results achieved with Zoho

One platform

Zoho One replaced a fragile chain of free and paid apps with a single subscription the team could grow into.

Guest context

Desk and CRM together mean every support or sales conversation starts with full booking and communication history.

Flexible CRM

Custom fields, blueprints, and dashboards mirror how luxury travel sales actually work - not generic B2B defaults.

Responsive support

When custom behaviour was needed, Zoho's support team delivered workable code and clear setup steps within days.

"Zoho One really is a one-stop shop for us. Because CRM holds our core data, tying in campaigns and desk means we finally see the full guest journey - not scattered threads across ten different logins."

- Co-founder, boutique luxury travel platform
