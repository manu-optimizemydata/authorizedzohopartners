E-commerce Case Study · Multi-Channel Retail Brand

How Zoho unified sales, stock, and finance for a multi-channel e-commerce brand

An online-first retail brand selling across its own site, marketplaces, and ad platforms replaced days of manual spreadsheet work with one connected Zoho stack-bringing storefront, inventory, accounting, and analytics into a single live view of the business.

  • Category: E-commerce Case Study
  • Industry: Online Retail & D2C Brands
  • Region: Global
  • Platform: Zoho One ecosystem

End-to-end commerce, stock, and finance software for online-first retail brands

  • MinutesLive business view, not days of spreadsheets
  • AutomaticOrder, stock & finance stay in sync
  • Multi-currencySelling internationally, simplified
  • 1 DashboardReplacing 4+ disconnected tools

The business challenge

Like many online-first brands, the business grew across channels faster than its back-office reporting could keep up.

  • Sales data scattered across the storefront, marketplaces, and ad platforms
  • Inventory tracked separately from orders, with no single source of stock truth
  • Days spent each week manually pulling numbers into Excel just to answer "how’s the business doing"
  • Multi-currency sales reconciled by hand every month
  • Support tickets tracked in a tool disconnected from orders and customer history
  • No shared, live view of the business for the wider team

What Zoho delivered

Zoho connected the storefront, stock, accounts, and reporting on one platform-from order to shelf to invoice to dashboard.

Zoho Commerce

Runs the storefront and order management, the system of record for every sale.

  • Storefront and order management
  • Multi-channel order capture
  • Customer accounts and order history
  • Integrated checkout

Zoho Inventory

Keeps stock in sync across every sales channel, not just the main storefront.

  • Multi-channel stock tracking
  • Reorder points and stock alerts
  • Warehouse and location visibility
  • Syncs automatically with orders

Zoho Books

Handles accounting and multi-currency reconciliation for brands selling internationally.

  • Multi-currency invoicing and accounting
  • Automatic reconciliation with orders
  • GST/VAT-ready reporting
  • Expense and bank reconciliation

Zoho Analytics

Blends storefront, ad, and support data into one live dashboard instead of a weekly Excel exercise.

  • Blends storefront, ad, and support data
  • Live dashboards instead of manual pulls
  • Shared reporting across the team
  • Trend and channel performance analysis

Results achieved with Zoho

Sales visibility

Sales, ad spend, and support data blend into one live dashboard instead of a weekly manual export.

Inventory

Stock stays in sync across every channel automatically, instead of being reconciled by hand.

Finance

Multi-currency sales stop being a monthly accounting headache.

Team

The wider team works from the same live numbers instead of one person’s spreadsheet.

"A B2C e-commerce brand factory selling skincare and accessories internationally had its co-founder spending 2-5 full days a week manually analyzing data across Shopify, Google Analytics, Zendesk, and Facebook Ads in Excel. After connecting everything into Zoho Analytics, that dropped to a few minutes, with 10+ team members now using shared reports."

— Real Zoho Analytics case study: Ecom Ventures
