Automotive Parts Case Study · Parts Distributor & Dealer

How Zoho connected sales, stock, and purchasing for an auto-parts distributor

An auto-parts distributor juggling a large, fast-moving catalogue across multiple locations replaced manual stock tracking with one connected Zoho system-covering customer and dealer relationships, real-time parts stock, and supplier purchasing.

The business challenge

Like many parts distributors, the business grew its catalogue and locations faster than its back-office visibility could keep up.

  • Large, fast-moving parts catalogue with no single source of stock truth
  • Purchase orders to multiple suppliers tracked manually
  • Customer and dealer relationships managed separately from stock and orders
  • Slow-moving dead stock tying up cash without visibility
  • Multiple locations or subsidiaries, with no consolidated view
  • Sales, quotes, and purchasing run as three disconnected manual processes

What Zoho delivered

Zoho connected customer relationships, real-time stock, and supplier purchasing on one platform-from quote to shelf to supplier bill.

Zoho CRM

Manages customer and dealer relationships, quotes, and orders in one pipeline.

  • Customer and dealer relationship tracking
  • Quotes and order management
  • Sales pipeline visibility
  • Follow-up and communication history

Zoho Inventory

Gives real-time visibility into a large, fast-moving parts catalogue across every location.

  • Real-time parts stock tracking
  • Reorder points on real data
  • Multi-location stock visibility
  • Slow-moving stock identification

Zoho Books

Connects purchasing and supplier bills directly to accounting, across locations.

  • Purchase orders and supplier bills
  • Multi-location accounting
  • Automated reconciliation
  • Financial reporting across subsidiaries

Results achieved with Zoho

Stock

Slow-moving stock becomes visible instead of invisible, freeing up cash tied in dead inventory.

Purchasing

Reordering happens on real data instead of guesswork.

Operations

Sales, quotes, and purchasing stop being three separate manual processes.
