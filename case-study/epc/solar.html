EPC Case Study · Solar & Renewable Energy

How Zoho unified sales, projects, and finance for a growing solar EPC company

A mid-size solar installation business replaced disconnected spreadsheets and manual follow-ups with the Zoho platform-gaining visibility from lead to commissioning across residential and commercial projects.

  • Category: EPC Case Study
  • Industry: Solar EPC & Installation
  • Region: India
  • Platform: Zoho One ecosystem

The business challenge

Like many fast-growing solar EPC firms, the company faced rising enquiry volume-but operations could not keep pace with manual processes.

  • Leads from website, referrals, and channel partners tracked in spreadsheets with no single source of truth
  • Site surveys, load assessments, and quotations prepared offline-slowing response times
  • Project stages (design, procurement, installation, net metering) managed separately from sales data
  • Inventory of panels, inverters, and mounting hardware not linked to active projects
  • Invoicing, advances, and GST compliance handled outside the sales pipeline
  • Leadership lacked real-time visibility into conversion rates and project backlog

What Zoho delivered

Zoho connected the full solar EPC lifecycle on one integrated platform-from first enquiry through installation, billing, and reporting.

Zoho CRM

Centralized every residential and commercial enquiry with automated assignment, follow-up reminders, and pipeline stages tailored to solar sales.

  • Lead capture from web forms, calls, and partner channels
  • Custom fields for capacity (kW), roof type, and location
  • Automated tasks for site survey scheduling
  • WhatsApp and email communication from the record

Zoho Projects

Turned won deals into structured installation projects with milestones for design approval, procurement, installation, and commissioning.

  • Project templates per system size and customer type
  • Task ownership for design, electrical, and field teams
  • Document storage for layouts and compliance files
  • Timeline visibility for operations managers

Zoho Books & Inventory

Linked quotations, purchase orders, and customer invoices to CRM deals-giving finance and operations a shared view of project economics.

  • GST-ready invoicing and payment tracking
  • Stock levels for panels, inverters, and accessories
  • Project-wise costing and margin visibility
  • Bank reconciliation and financial reporting

Zoho Analytics

Gave leadership live dashboards on enquiry sources, conversion by region, average project size, and installation backlog.

  • Sales funnel and win-rate reporting
  • Project completion and delay tracking
  • Revenue forecast by pipeline stage
  • Team performance across branches

Results achieved with Zoho

Sales & pre-sales

Zoho CRM reduced lead response time and standardized quotations-helping the sales team handle more enquiries without losing quality.

Project delivery

Zoho Projects aligned field, design, and procurement teams on shared milestones-cutting delays between survey, install, and handover.

Finance & inventory

Zoho Books and Inventory eliminated duplicate data entry between operations and accounts, improving billing accuracy and stock planning.

Leadership visibility

Zoho Analytics provided a single pane of glass for pipeline health, active installations, and revenue-supporting faster, data-driven decisions.

“Before Zoho, our teams worked in silos-sales did not know project status, and finance chased information manually. Today, Zoho gives us one connected view from enquiry to commissioning. That is the difference.”

- Operations Director, Solar EPC Company (India)
