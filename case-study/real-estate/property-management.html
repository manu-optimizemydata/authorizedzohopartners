Real Estate Case Study · Property Management & Brokerage

How Zoho automated rent invoicing and owner reporting for a property management business

A property management company or brokerage tracking listings, tenants, and rent across spreadsheets and email replaced manual monthly billing runs with one connected Zoho system covering the full property lifecycle from listing to lease to renewal.

The business challenge

Like many property businesses, growth outpaced the manual processes tracking it.

  • Property listings tracked across spreadsheets, email threads, and phone calls
  • No single view of a property’s lifecycle from listing to lease to renewal
  • Rent collection and owner payouts run as a manual monthly billing exercise
  • Maintenance requests handled ad hoc with no central record
  • Owner and tenant relationship history scattered across inboxes
  • Admin overhead scales with every new property added

What Zoho delivered

Zoho brought the full property lifecycle into one system-from listing and lead through lease, rent, and renewal.

Zoho CRM

Tracks property listings, leads, and owner/tenant relationships in one searchable system.

  • Property listing management
  • Lead and enquiry tracking
  • Owner and tenant relationship history
  • Full lifecycle visibility, listing to renewal

Zoho Books

Automates rent invoicing and owner payouts instead of a manual monthly billing run.

  • Automated recurring rent invoicing
  • Owner payout tracking
  • Financial reporting per property
  • Replaces manual billing runs

Results achieved with Zoho

Finance

Rent invoicing and owner payouts run on autopilot instead of manual monthly effort.

Operations

Every property, tenant, and owner relationship lives in one searchable system instead of scattered email threads.
