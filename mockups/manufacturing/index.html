Who are we?

VELTRON Precision Pvt Ltd is a market leader in providing laser solutions to various industries. We aim to proactively engage with our clients as a technology partner. Our goal is to offer innovative solutions that meet the needs of our customers and establish lasting relationships with them. To maintain our position as a market leader, it is essential that we continuously innovate and keep pace with the latest advancements in laser technology. Our primary objective is to ensure complete customer satisfaction, and we have consistently worked towards achieving this goal. Founded in 2011 as a Private Limited Company, our headquarters are located in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.
