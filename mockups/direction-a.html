Who we are & what we do

Indian wear manufacturing, bright on the floor. Steady in bulk.

KORA is a family-led apparel manufacturer. We turn festive briefs into production-ready sarees, lehengas, kurtas, and colour-led knits with the oversight serious buying programmes require.

Women’s ethnic wear is our core — drape, zari, and colour matching leave little room for error — and the same discipline runs across men’s kurtas and kidswear. Cut-to-pack sits in-house; weaving, dyeing, and selected embroidery move through approved partners.
