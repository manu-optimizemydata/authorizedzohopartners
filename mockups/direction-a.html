Who we are & what we do

Knitwear manufacturing, built for scale. Managed with control.

KORA is a family-led apparel manufacturer. We turn product ideas into production-ready garments with the structure, consistency, and oversight serious buying programmes require.

Women’s wear is our core — fit, hand-feel, and finishing leave little room for error — and the same discipline runs across men’s and kidswear. Cut-to-pack sits in-house; spinning, knitting, dyeing, and selected prints move through approved partners.
